# DESIGN BRIEF — Aditya Jakhar Developer Portfolio

## 1. Project Overview

- **Subject**: Aditya Jakhar
- **Core Identity**: Computer Science Engineer · Full-Stack Developer · AI/ML Enthusiast
- **Positioning Statement**: *"Engineering Ideas. Building Impact."*
- **Brand Personality**: Ambitious, technical, creative, curious, detail-oriented.
- **Primary Objective**: Present Aditya Jakhar as a high-craft software engineer who builds real full-stack and AI-powered systems—distinctly positioned as an authentic individual engineer rather than a digital agency, freelancer template, or generic SaaS landing page.

---

## 2. Reference Visual Analysis & Synthesis

All four reference directions were analyzed to extract a unified visual grammar without copying any single layout directly:

| Reference | Key Visual Strengths Extracted | How It Shapes Aditya's Identity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reference 1** *(Cinematic Lighting & Atmosphere)* | Deep obsidian shadows illuminated by controlled crimson (`#B51220`), bright red (`#E52B22`), and warm orange (`#FF5A1F`) volumetric rim lighting; sleek minimal top navigation; subtle architectural vertical grid lines. | Establishes the dramatic studio atmosphere behind the hero composition and focal interactive states without resorting to noisy multi-color neon gradients. |
| **Reference 2** *(Strong Typography & Clean Grids)* | Condensed monumental `Bebas Neue` display scale paired with `Caveat` handwritten personal script (`Hello, I'm`), indexed `01 / 02 / 03` project rows, and crisp `1px` structural hairlines. | Provides engineering order, clear scannability, numbered section/project indexing, and human warmth via restrained handwritten annotations. |
| **Reference 3** *(Oversized Identity & Central Portrait)* | Commanding uppercase typographic presence, central portrait anchor, geometric registration marks (`+` / 4-point star), and high-contrast red directional CTA (`ArrowUpRight`). | Anchors the first viewport with unmistakable personal authorship (`ADITYA JAKHAR`) and decisive call-to-action hierarchy. |
| **Reference 4** *(Premium Editorial Composition)* | Full-width warm ivory (`#F2EDE7`) condensed header spanning above an asymmetric 3-zone hero stage (left discipline stack + quote, center crimson-halo portrait, right narrative + action links) framed by hairline rules. | Unifies monumental display type, central portrait placeholder, positioning statement, and animated capability statements into a balanced editorial stage. |

### Common Visual Characteristics Across All Four
1. **Extreme Scale Contrast**: Monumental condensed uppercase display type (`Bebas Neue`) juxtaposed against refined geometric sans-serif (`Manrope Variable`) micro-labels (`11px–13px`, wide tracking) and readable body copy.
2. **Dark Architectural Discipline**: `80–85%+` deep black/espresso surfaces (`#090909`, `#171313`, `#201919`) structured by `1px solid rgba(242, 237, 231, 0.14)` hairlines rather than heavy drop-shadow cards or blurred glass clutter.
3. **Controlled Warm Chromatic Accent**: Surgical use of Crimson (`#B51220`), Red (`#E52B22`), and Warm Orange (`#FF5A1F`) strictly around the focal portrait halo, primary CTAs, and active indicators.
4. **Editorial Human Touch**: Sparse `Caveat` script accents and clean typographic registration marks that signal craftsmanship.

---

## 3. Anti-Patterns Strictly Prohibited

- **No Agency Disguise**: First-person singular engineering voice (*"I architect and build..."*, never *"We deliver solutions"*).
- **No Fabricated Metrics or Portraits**: Zero invented years of experience, client counts, awards, or AI-generated face photos. Availability status renders strictly when explicitly verified (`verified: true`).
- **No Generic AI / SaaS Clichés**: No purple/cyan gradients, no floating glassmorphic dashboard widgets, no emoji bullets, and no unmotivated particle noise.

---

## 4. First-Screen (Hero) Art Direction

The Hero (`01. HERO`) is engineered as an editorial 100dvh composition:
1. **Minimal Top Navigation**: Brand monogram + name (`AJ` / `ADITYA JAKHAR`), indexed section links (`01`–`07`), GitHub icon/link, and compact `Contact` CTA, with accessible mobile drawer on small viewports.
2. **Monumental Display Banner**: Full-width fluid `Bebas Neue` `ADITYA JAKHAR` headline in warm ivory (`#F2EDE7`) with staggered masked line entrance, layered subtly above the central portrait frame.
3. **Asymmetric Three-Column Editorial Stage**:
   - **Left Rail (Identity & Positioning)**: Handwritten `Caveat` *"Hello, I'm"* accent, structured engineering roles (`Computer Science Engineer`, `Full-Stack Developer`, `AI/ML Enthusiast`), and positioning manifesto *"Engineering Ideas. Building Impact."*
   - **Center Stage (Deliberate Portrait Composition)**: Sculpted `4:5` architectural studio frame backed by a radial `#B51220` / `#FF5A1F` volumetric halo, subtle corner registration crop marks, chiaroscuro silhouette geometry, and explicit `PORTRAIT PLACEHOLDER` specification ready for seamless 1-line swap when Aditya's photograph is provided.
   - **Right Rail (Live Capability Reveal & Actions)**: Refined Motion-powered chromatic sweep text-reveal cycling through the four core engineering statements (with pause-on-hover/focus and `prefers-reduced-motion` support), magnetic primary CTA (`Explore Projects`), and direct `GitHub` + `Contact` actions.
4. **Bottom Architectural Index Bar**: Hairline-framed footer strip previewing the three flagship engineering builds (`01 AI-Loan`, `02 LawLane AI`, `03 Campus Lost & Found`) and scroll cue.
